## [CompMotifs](https://compmotifs.com/)

I co-lead CompMotifs, a community that brings researchers together at hackathons to work on computational problems in the natural sciences.

## [Proteina-Complexa](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/genair/proteina-complexa/)

A generative model for protein complexes. We studied how scaling the training data and giving the model more time to search affect its ability to design protein binders.

## [ProteinWorkshop](https://github.com/a-r-j/ProteinWorkshop)

A benchmark suite for comparing what machine learning models learn from protein structures, with shared datasets and evaluation tasks.

## [AtomWorks](https://rosettacommons.github.io/atomworks/latest/)

A Python toolkit for reading biomolecular structures and preparing them for machine learning. It gives researchers a common way to represent proteins, nucleic acids, and small molecules.

## [RFD4-Proteina](https://rfd4-proteina.org/)

A collaboration between the Institute for Protein Design and NVIDIA exploring how latent reasoning can improve biomolecular design.

## [BioHacks 2023]({{ '/projects/biohack/' | relative_url }})

BioHack London and the online BioxML Hackathon brought people working in machine learning and the life sciences together in 2023.
